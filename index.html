Background

What is
Business Intelligence (BI)?

BI helps organizations turn raw data into actionable insight. — provided you already know exactly what to ask.

1
Frame The business side defines a specific question.
2
Shape Engineers merge multiple tables into one big combined table for cross-querying.
3
Read The business side reads charts, makes decisions, and outputs reports.
BI is like assembling a puzzle when you already know the picture on the box — the outline of the answer is already set.
But There's a Catch

Every Answer

BI handles one known question well. But real investigation is rarely just one question — each answer sparks the next. Let's see what that looks like in practice.

Where This Workflow Breaks

Why Traditional BI Workflow
Can't Support Exploratory Analysis

01

Heavy dependence on expert knowledge

Analysts must identify all relevant data sources upfront and coordinate with the database team to prepare them. Miss or misjudge — the conclusion skews.

02

Combining data is slow and expensive

Linking multiple data sources on a large dataset is slow and memory-heavy. Step 3 alone needs to connect 193M papers with 51M paper-institution links.

03

The data model is frozen

Once the data structure is designed, it can't be changed mid-analysis. Discovered a new dimension? You can only restart from scratch — you can't modify while exploring.

04

Previous work can't be reused

In the case study, the same paper-institution linkage was computed 3 separate times. Every round starts fresh; the previous work is thrown away.

The Bottleneck

Two Walls Standing
in the Way

Exploratory BI sounds great in theory — but on real data, two practical problems make it impossible without new techniques.

1
Data cannot be reused

In traditional databases, each query starts from raw data. When exploration adds a new dimension, the system recomputes everything from scratch — including work it already did in the previous round.

In the Russia case: the paper–institution linkage was computed 3 separate times across 3 rounds. Each round threw away the previous result.

This isn't a software bug — it's a fundamental limitation of how traditional databases work. They have no concept of “building on previous results.”

2
Full computation is too slow

Linking millions of records across multiple data sources creates billions of combinations. Traditional databases try to compute every single one — and either run out of memory or take hours.

Real numbers: OpenAIRE has 17.9M papers, but each paper can cite many others — when you expand all citation pairs, the result explodes to 1.4 billion rows. Even a simple pattern search on 10K records takes 4+ minutes.

Interactive exploration needs answers in seconds. Full computation simply can't deliver that.

These two walls are why exploratory analysis has stayed a nice idea — until now. Our solution: a data model that remembers previous work, and smart sampling that skips unnecessary computation.
The Key Innovation

Our Answer:
Smart Sampling

You don't need to count every grain of sand to know how much is on the beach.
The same principle applies to data analysis.

Like election polls — you don't need to ask every voter to predict the result.
A well-chosen sample gives 99%+ accurate counts and totals in seconds.
For Extracting Data

Instead of finding every matching record in a massive dataset, sample a representative subset. The full search would take minutes or hours — sampling takes seconds.

For Linking Data Together

Instead of computing all possible combinations when connecting two datasets, sample from the result directly. The 1.4-billion-row explosion? Never even gets constructed.

Speed Seconds instead of hours
Accuracy > 99% for COUNT, SUM, and trend queries
Fair ✓ every record has equal chance
Composable ✓ results chain across rounds

为什么传统 BI 工作流
撑不起探索式分析？

01

重度依赖专家经验

分析师必须预先识别所有可能相关的数据源，和数据库管理员反复沟通才能准备数据。遗漏或误判，结论就偏。

刚才案例里，论文与机构的关联计算被重复做了 3 次。每一轮都从零开始，上一轮的工作全部丢掉。

现实困境

两堵墙
挡在探索的路上

探索式 BI 的理念很好，但落到真实数据上，有两个实际问题让它难以实现。

1
上下文太长

Agent 做探索式分析不是一次问答，而是一串连续决策：问题拆解、查询语句、中间结果、异常分支和解释口径都会不断累积，最终形成越来越长的上下文。

俄乌案例中：三轮追问会累积国家口径、机构映射、论文筛选、合作关系和时间窗口等信息。后续每一步都要从冗长历史里找出真正相关的约束。

上下文越长，成本越高、响应越慢，关键约束也越容易被稀释或遗忘。

2
全量计算太慢

把数百万条记录跨多个数据源关联，会产生数十亿种组合。传统数据库试图逐一计算——要么内存爆掉，要么跑上几个小时。

实际数据：OpenAIRE 有 1790 万篇论文，但每篇可以引用多篇其他论文——展开所有引用对后，结果膨胀到 14 亿行。即便是 1 万条记录的简单查找，也要跑 4 分钟以上。

交互式探索需要秒级响应，全量计算根本做不到。

正是这两堵墙，让探索式分析一直只是个好想法。我们的解决方案：使用BI压缩上下文，加上聪明地采样跳过不必要的计算。
核心创新

我们的解决方案：
聪明的采样

你不需要数完沙滩上的每一粒沙，就能知道沙滩有多大。
数据分析也是同样的道理。

就像选举民调——不用问每一个选民也能预测结果。
精心设计的抽样，几秒钟就能给出计数和趋势准确率 99% 以上的答案。
提取数据时

不用在海量数据中找出每一条匹配记录，只抽一个有代表性的子集。全量搜索要几分钟甚至几小时——抽样只要几秒。

关联数据时

不用算出所有的组合，直接从结果中抽样。那个 14 亿行的爆炸结果？压根不会被构造出来。

速度 秒级 不再是小时级

速度与精度，双双过关

在社交网络基准数据集上，分三个规模测试：33 万、100 万、360 万个节点。

加速倍数 36.7× 40 秒 vs. 25 分钟
计数准确率 < 1% 所有查询的误差
峰值准确率 ~0.01% 误差极小且稳定
大规模完成率 10 / 10 不采样的话：10 个查询只能完成 1 个
不仅仅是更快——是唯一的办法

在最大的数据集上，传统方式 10 个查询有 9 个直接失败——要么内存不足，要么跑了 2 小时还没出结果。采样方式 10 个全部完成。

规模越大，采样不是"锦上添花"，而是"唯一出路"。

抽多少样本就够了？

一开始精度提升很快，之后趋于平稳。超过某个点后，多抽样本对精度几乎没帮助，但耗时增长 10 倍。

我们找到了最佳平衡点：够快，能交互使用；够准，能支撑真实决策。

分析能力层

让 NeuGBI 跨平台可用

我们为 NeuGBI 提供了一套完整的 Skill Suite——任何 Agent 平台加载这套技能，都能直接运行 NeuGBI 的探索式分析。

① 数据库 & Schema 理解

基于 schema 与样本数据，理解每一类顶点、每一种边的语义——为后续查询打下准确基础。

② 实体对齐

把用户问题中的实体（"俄罗斯"、"2022 年"…）准确映射到数据库字段，避免误解题意。

③ 问题拆解

把一个宽泛的问题拆成若干子问题，降低单步复杂度，让分析能从多个角度同时展开。

④ 查询生成

针对每个子问题，生成合适的查询，交给计算层（提取 / 拼接 / 查看）执行。

⑤ 对照组设计

为查询结果与结论自主设计对照实验——验证发现、增强可信度。"俄罗斯跌了，那其他国家呢？"

⑥ 探索式分析 Pipeline

把前 5 项串成一条迭代探索链：每一轮的发现自动引出下一轮的问题和查询。

用户只需要抛出一个问题；Skill Suite 自动完成"拆解 → 查询 → 验证 → 下一轮"的完整闭环，最终产出一份结构化的分析报告。
